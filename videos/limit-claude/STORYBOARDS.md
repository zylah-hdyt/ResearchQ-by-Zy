## 1. Cinematic 3D: "The Reservoir"
A single object the whole way through, like an Apple product film: a glass capsule holding a glowing amber core (Claude's orange). Black studio, rim light, haze, slow and expensive camera moves. Three.js via `@remotion/three`.

| Beat | Shot | Camera | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | A glass capsule floats in the dark and its amber core shrinks quickly to almost empty, with the light dimming. Thin label "LIMIT 0%". | Slow macro push-in, slight dutch tilt | Low drone, sub-hit on "habis" |
| B2 | The capsule splits into two forms: a **ring** (left) and **seven pillars** (right). Label "2 BATAS". | Pull-back reveal, parallax | Whoosh at the split |
| B3 | Close on the ring: a dial of 5 glowing segments (5 hours) used up one by one. Label "CURRENT SESSION · 5 JAM". | Orbit 40° around the ring, rack focus | Soft ticks per segment |
| B4 | Light sweeps around the ring and all 5 segments refill. "RESET OTOMATIS". | Quick dolly-in to the flash | Riser into a ding |
| B5 | The camera tracks across 7 glass pillars (Mon–Sun); light moves across them one by one; a separate small cycle icon. "WEEKLY LIMIT · JADWAL RESET SENDIRI". | Lateral truck plus crane up | Low swell, ending on a sub tail |

Transitions: object morphs (no hard cuts), plus a light flare at B4.

## 2. Kinetic Typography: "Editorial Punch"
Type is the only visual. Giant condensed sans (Anton) against italic serif (Instrument Serif), cream / ink / orange. Every key word lands on its VO syllable.

| Beat | Layout | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | "PERNAH NGGAK," stacks in; **LIMIT** fills the screen; **HABIS** drains: letters drop out from the bottom via a mask, leaving a progress bar at 0. | Masked line reveals, scale punch on "habis" |
| B2 | A giant **2** slams to full height, then splits vertically into two columns: "BATAS / penggunaan". | Number morph, split-screen wipe |
| B3 | Background flips to ink. "*pertama*" (serif) → **CURRENT SESSION** in tracked caps → a **5:00:00** counter running down → "JAM". | Character stagger, counter, horizontal marquee |
| B4 | **RESET** whose letters spin backwards (rewind), then "otomatis" snaps under it. | Reverse-letter rotation, flash |
| B5 | Background flips to orange. "*kedua*" → **WEEKLY LIMIT** → a 7-letter row "S S R K J S M" filling in one by one → "jadwal reset sendiri". | Grid fill, final type lock-up |

Transitions: hard type-driven cuts, color-block wipes, masked pushes. SFX: whip, click, switch.

## 3. Cinematic Documentary: "Batas Yang Tak Terlihat" (The Invisible Limit)
Netflix-style docuseries look: Unsplash photography, 2.39:1-style letterboxing inside the vertical frame, film grain, slow parallax, chapter cards, serif subtitles.

| Beat | Image | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Someone working at a desk at night (overhead shot) → a laptop glowing in the dark | Slow push-in, grain, cold grade; subtitle |
| B2 | Clock face and calendar, split into two frames | Diptych slide-in, "BAB I / BAB II" |
| B3 | Hourglass in the sand | Chapter card "I. CURRENT SESSION", deep parallax, timecode 05:00:00 |
| B4 | A wall of clocks | Hard cut plus a film burn (light leak), "reset otomatis" |
| B5 | Planner/calendar | Chapter card "II. WEEKLY LIMIT", slow tilt, fade out to black |

SFX: projector/shutter, low ambient room tone, whoosh on chapter cards.

## 4. Futuristic Tech Interface: "Usage OS"
A holographic HUD floating in a field of 3D particles (Three.js). Glass panels (glassmorphism), gauges, scanlines, a data ticker. Cyan and violet, nothing orange, so it never reads like a recolored version of 1 or 2.

| Beat | Interface |
|---|---|
| B1 | Boot sequence → "USAGE" gauge needle drops 72% → 100% → red "LIMIT REACHED" warning with glitch |
| B2 | The panel splits into two glass cards: "LIMIT_01" and "LIMIT_02" fly in with depth |
| B3 | Card 01 enlarges: radial 5-hour timeline, "SESSION · 5H WINDOW", 04:59:59 countdown |
| B4 | Scan beam → "RESET ✓" → gauge back to 0% |
| B5 | Card 02: a 7-day bar chart (S–M) filling up, "WEEKLY · RESET: JADWAL SENDIRI" |

Transitions: glitch slice, panel zoom-through, particle drift. SFX: UI clicks, switch, glitch, ding.

## 5. 3D Paper & Mixed Media: "Kertas Kerja" (Working Paper)
Stop-motion at 12 fps (stepped frames), crumpled paper textures, cut-outs with real shadows, hand-drawn scribbles, a photo collage (alarm clock, hourglass, calendar) cut into stickers, and paper cards that flip in CSS 3D.

| Beat | Collage |
|---|---|
| B1 | A paper "LIMIT" meter torn off; a crumpled paper ball rolls in; scribble "habis?!" |
| B2 | Two index cards slide in from the left and right, each with a large stamped "1" and "2" |
| B3 | Card 1 flips over (3D): an hourglass cut-out plus a 5-hour paper clock whose wedges are cut one by one |
| B4 | Rubber stamp "RESET" thumps; the wedges spring back |
| B5 | Card 2: a calendar cut-out, 7 sticky notes stuck on one by one, hand-drawn circle around "jadwal sendiri" |

Transitions: paper wipes (a torn edge sweeping across), page-turn. SFX: page-turn, paper rustle, stamp thump, pop.
