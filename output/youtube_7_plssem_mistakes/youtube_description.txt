7 PLS-SEM Mistakes Most Students Make (Hair et al., 2022)

Using PLS-SEM for your thesis? There's a good chance you're making at least one of these seven mistakes: the simultaneous F-test, the GoF index, the "small sample" excuse, Fornell-Larcker, the Sobel test, reliability that's too high, and one universal R² cutoff.

✅ WHAT TO DO INSTEAD
• R² together with the significance of each path, not an F-test
• R², effect sizes, and predictive power, not GoF
• Statistical power (inverse square root method), not "my sample is small"
• HTMT (below 0.90, or 0.85 for distinct constructs), not Fornell-Larcker
• Bootstrap the indirect effect, not the Sobel test
• Reliability between 0.70 and 0.90; above 0.95 usually means redundant items
• Judge R² in context, compared with similar studies
