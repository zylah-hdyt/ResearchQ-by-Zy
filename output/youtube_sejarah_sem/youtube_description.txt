🇬🇧 How did structural equation modeling begin? This 3-minute story goes from Spearman's hidden factor (1904) and Sewall Wright's guinea pigs (1921) to Haavelmo, Duncan, Jöreskog's LISREL and Wold's soft modeling, the two types of SEM (CB-SEM vs PLS-SEM), and the software behind almost every thesis, from LISREL to SmartPLS 4.

✅ RINGKASAN / QUICK SUMMARY
• 1904 Spearman: analisis faktor (mengukur yang tak terlihat lewat yang terlihat)
• 1921 Wright: analisis jalur (panah antarvariabel menjadi persamaan)
• 1940-an–60-an Haavelmo & Duncan: sistem persamaan dan analisis jalur di ilmu sosial
• 1970-an Jöreskog: CB-SEM dan LISREL; Wold: PLS-SEM (soft modeling)
• CB-SEM → menguji kecocokan teori; PLS-SEM → menjelaskan dan memprediksi
• Software: LISREL, LVPLS, EQS, Amos, PLS-Graph, Mplus, SmartPLS, WarpPLS, lavaan, SmartPLS 4
