The History of SEM: From Spearman's g to SmartPLS | Sub Indo

How did structural equation modeling begin? This 3-minute story goes from Spearman's hidden factor (1904) and Sewall Wright's guinea pigs (1921) to Haavelmo, Duncan, Karl Jöreskog's LISREL and Herman Wold's soft modeling, the two types of SEM (CB-SEM vs PLS-SEM), and the software that made SEM part of almost every thesis.

🇮🇩 Bagaimana SEM lahir? Kisah 3 menit dari faktor "g" Spearman (1904), marmut Sewall Wright (1921), sampai Jöreskog (LISREL) dan Wold (PLS). Plus dua jenis SEM (CB-SEM vs PLS-SEM) dan software-nya: LISREL, EQS, LVPLS, Amos, PLS-Graph, Mplus, SmartPLS, WarpPLS, lavaan.
